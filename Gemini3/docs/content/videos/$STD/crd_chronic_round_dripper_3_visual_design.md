### BEAT 1: The Crypto Meltdown (0:00-0:08)

**Visual Concept:** Chaotic amateur panic captures authentic degen energy
**Viral Visual:** Shocked face looking down at pants - peak meme potential

#### Subject Description

**VISUAL:** Disheveled white male, 28 years old, bloodshot eyes, messy brown hair, wearing stained gray Solana hoodie, cargo shorts, nervous twitching, constantly checking phone, pale skin, stubble beard, dark circles under eyes, thin build

**VOICE:** Frantic high-pitched voice, speaks rapidly with panic, occasional voice cracks, heavy breathing between sentences, yells profanities

#### Environmental Design
- Location: Messy bedroom/basement setup, multiple monitors showing red crypto charts
- Props: Gaming chair, energy drink cans, tissues, crumpled papers, USB cables
- Time/Weather: Late afternoon, window blinds creating harsh streaks
- Style: Realistic amateur bedroom aesthetic

#### Composition
- Framing: Handheld medium shot, slightly shaky to convey anxiety
- Depth: Monitors in background, subject in mid-ground, debris in foreground
- Leading Lines: Monitor edges and cables guide to subject's face
- Negative Space: Cluttered - intentionally overwhelming

#### Lighting Setup

**Key Light:** Harsh side window light, 5000K, creates dramatic shadows on face
**Fill:** Blue monitor glow on opposite side, cold digital tint
**Contrast:** High 6:1 ratio for stress and chaos
**Practicals:** Multiple monitor screens visible, phone screen bright

#### Camera Specifications
- Shot: Medium shot from slightly below eye level
- Lens: 35mm equivalent for natural but slightly uncomfortable perspective
- Movement: Subtle handheld shake, no deliberate camera moves
- Motivation: Amateur panic documentation feel

#### Physics Elements
- Primary: Phone vibration in hand, natural arm gestures
- Secondary: Hair movement from head scratching, fabric wrinkles
- Materials: Soft fabric of hoodie, hard plastic of phone
- Animation: Realistic nervous energy and fidgeting

#### Color Design
- Palette: Desaturated warm mess (brown/gray/red charts)
- Psychology: Chaos and financial disaster
- Grading: Slightly overexposed highlights, crushed shadows
- Style Match: Amateur TikTok authenticity
