### RED FLAG ANALYSIS

**Potential Issues Identified:**
- ❌ **Explicit Content**: Sexual references may violate platform policies
- ❌ **Complex Medical Terminology**: May affect audience comprehension
- ⚠️ **Heavy Dialogue Load**: Beats 4 & 7 are text-heavy
- ⚠️ **Character Consistency**: Must maintain degen patient appearance

**Mitigation Strategies:**
1. **Content Sanitization**: Replace explicit terms with euphemisms
2. **Dialogue Compression**: Prioritize most impactful lines
3. **Visual Support**: Use graphics/props to convey information
4. **Character Bible**: Lock exact appearance descriptions
