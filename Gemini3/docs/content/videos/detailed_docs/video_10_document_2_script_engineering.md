## INITIAL CONCEPT NOTES

This is a continuous story showing a parent (Linda) and adult child (Tom) where the tech-savvy younger generation must explain AI to their concerned parent. The role reversal creates comedy through relatable family dynamics while educating about AI capabilities and investment opportunities. The progression builds from confusion to fear to excited understanding.

**Creative Direction for Refinement:** Consider adding more physical comedy in parent reactions, enhanced visual demonstrations of AI capabilities, and stronger celebration of understanding in the final beat.
