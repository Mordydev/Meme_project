## Production Recommendations

### Quick Wins
1. **Start with**: Medium energy + TITCOIN memes
2. **Platform priority**: TikTok first (highest viral potential)
3. **Timing**: Post during meme hours (9pm-11pm EST)

### Testing Order
1. Energy levels (biggest impact)
2. Meme selection (community preference)
3. Platform optimizations (fine-tuning)
4. Dialogue variations (final polish)

### Success Indicators
- Immediate laughs within 2 seconds
- Comments saying "one of us"
- Reshares with personal stories
- Both communities engaging

## Character Consistency Note

**CRITICAL**: In ALL variations, maintain exact character descriptions:
- **Jess**: "Jessica Martinez, 26-year-old female, long brown hair with blonde highlights in casual waves..."
- **Gemini**: "Gemini 3 AI, ageless androgynous leaning feminine appearance, translucent holographic form..."

Never use shortcuts. Always include complete descriptions.
