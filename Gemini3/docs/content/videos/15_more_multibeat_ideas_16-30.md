## Production Notes for Extended Multi-Beat Videos

### Advanced Timing Considerations:
- 2-3 beats (16-24s): Quick stories, single narrative arc
- 4-5 beats (32-40s): Full episodes, complete transformations
- 6 beats (48s): Mini-movies, complex narratives

### Structural Innovations:
- **Parallel narratives**: Show multiple outcomes simultaneously
- **Time jumps**: Past/present/future in one video
- **Format parodies**: Use familiar TV/movie formats
- **Extended character arcs**: Development across all beats

### Viral Optimization for Longer Content:
- Hook every 8 seconds to maintain retention
- Build to satisfying conclusion
- Multiple shareable moments throughout
- Clear story structure viewers can follow

These extended formats allow for richer storytelling while maintaining the punchy, shareable nature required for viral success. Each video can be cut into smaller segments for different platforms while working as complete narratives.
