# Prompt 4: Integration Architecture & Production Assembly

## Your Mission
Synthesize all elements into cohesive production blueprints. Create seamless flow between beats through precise audio bridging, visual transitions, and platform-optimized delivery strategies.

## Why Integration Matters
Most multi-beat videos fail because they feel like separate clips stitched together. This prompt focuses on:
- Creating seamless narrative flow between 8-second beats
- Leveraging Veo 3's audio continuity for emotional bridges
- Building platform-specific versions from master content
- Ensuring character and story consistency across all beats

## Critical Consistency Requirements
**Character Descriptions in Every Beat:**
- NEVER reference previous beats ("same as beat 1")
- Include complete character description: gender, age, appearance
- Copy EXACT details word-for-word for consistency
- This applies to ALL beats in your integration
