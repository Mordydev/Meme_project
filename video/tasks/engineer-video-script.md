# Engineer Video Script (Natural Dialogue & Timing)

## ⚠️ CRITICAL EXECUTION NOTICE ⚠️

**THIS IS AN EXECUTABLE WORKFLOW - NOT REFERENCE MATERIAL**

When this task is invoked:

1. **NO ALL CAPS IN DIALOGUE** - Prevents letter-by-letter audio reading
2. **8-SECOND CONSTRAINTS** - Every word must earn its place
3. **NATURAL SPEECH REQUIRED** - Dialogue must sound real when spoken
4. **STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION** - Work with user on each beat

## Mission
Engineer precise dialogue and audio scripts that work within 8-second constraints while maintaining natural delivery and viral potential.

## Prerequisites
- Validated concept with beat structure
- Character profiles defined
- Visual blueprint (if available)

## Phase 2: Timing Constraints Education

Present timing reality:
```
8-SECOND TIMING RULES:

WORD LIMITS BY SCENARIO:
- Solo speaker, normal pace: 12-18 words
- Solo with pauses/reactions: 10-15 words
- Two speakers exchanging: 8-10 words each
- With significant SFX: 12-14 words total
- With action + dialogue: 10-15 words max

DELIVERY SPEEDS:
- Relaxed/thoughtful: 2 words/second
- Normal conversation: 2.5 words/second
- Rapid/excited: 3 words/second
- With emotion: -20% speed

CRITICAL: Every word must advance story or emotion

Understood? (yes/questions):
```

## Phase 8: Audio Formatting Validation

**CRITICAL CHECK:**

```
AUDIO FORMATTING VERIFICATION:

DIALOGUE TEXT CHECK:
□ NO ALL CAPS in spoken dialogue
□ Visual text/logos can have caps
□ Emphasis through delivery notes, not CAPS
□ Natural capitalization throughout

PROBLEM EXAMPLES TO AVOID:
❌ "THIS IS AMAZING!" (reads as T-H-I-S)
❌ "OH MY GOD!" (reads as O-H M-Y G-O-D)
❌ "WHAT?!" (reads as W-H-A-T)

CORRECT EXAMPLES:
✅ "This is amazing!" + Delivery: excited shout
✅ "Oh my god!" + Delivery: shocked exclamation
✅ "What?!" + Delivery: confused outburst

All dialogue properly formatted? (yes/fix):
```
