## AUTHENTICITY MARKERS

For Natural Dialogue:
- Interruptions and overlaps
- "Um" and "uh" placement
- Breathing and pauses
- Genuine reactions (gasps, laughs)
- Incomplete sentences
- Natural speech patterns

For Voice Consistency:
- Same character = Same voice characteristics
- Maintain tone/pace/pitch across beats
- Keep vocal quirks consistent (stutters, pauses)
- Energy level appropriate to story moment
- Different characters = Distinctly different voices

For Animated Dialogue:
- Character-specific speech patterns
- Exaggerated but believable reactions
- Personality in every word
- Emotional vocal variety
- Consistent voice acting style

For Different Content Types:
- **Vlogs**: Direct address, personal asides
- **Animation**: Personality-driven delivery
- **Trailers**: Building intensity, epic moments
- **Interviews**: Question repetition, thinking pauses
- **News**: Authoritative delivery, clear enunciation
- **Ads**: Enthusiasm without feeling forced

**Dialogue Quality:**
□ Does dialogue sound natural when spoken aloud?
□ Are character voices distinct and memorable?
□ Is there a quotable/shareable line?
□ Do conversations overlap naturally (if applicable)?
□ Can all dialogue be spoken in 8 seconds?
□ Is word count appropriate (12-18 words)?

**Entertainment Value:**
□ Would you watch this if you saw it?
□ Is there a clear emotional journey?
□ Does it respect the audience's time?
□ Is the payoff worth the setup?

**Technical Feasibility:**
□ Can each beat realistically fit in 8 seconds?
□ Are audio layers clearly defined?
□ Is character/setting description specific enough?
□ Will Veo 3's strengths be utilized?

**If any criterion fails:** Rewrite focusing on entertainment and natural dialogue. Remember: people share what makes them feel something.
