## Production Notes for All Multi-Beat Videos

### Key Success Factors:
1. **Character Consistency**: Full descriptions repeated in each beat
2. **Scene Variety**: Mix of locations and perspectives 
3. **Building Momentum**: Each beat escalates the story
4. **Clear Audio**: 12-18 words per beat maximum
5. **Viral Moments**: Multiple shareable points throughout

### Structure Options Used:
- **Continuous**: Same character(s) throughout (Videos 2, 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 15)
- **Compilation**: Different scenes/characters exploring theme (Videos 1, 4, 7, 12)
- **Hybrid**: Mix of both approaches (Videos 5, 14)

### Platform Optimization:
- **TikTok**: Front-loaded hooks, quick cuts between beats
- **YouTube Shorts**: Complete story arcs with satisfying endings
- **Instagram Reels**: Aesthetic scenes, shareable moments
- **Twitter**: Quotable dialogue, meme-able reactions

These multi-beat videos allow for richer storytelling while maintaining the punch and shareability needed for viral success.
